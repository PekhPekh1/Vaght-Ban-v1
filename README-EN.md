​📖 About The Project
​Vaqt-Ban (Persian for "Time-Keeper" or "Time-Guard") is a lightweight and efficient tool designed to help you monitor, manage, and optimize how you spend your time. Whether you are tracking daily tasks, setting reminders, or limiting screen/activity time, Vaqt-Ban provides a clean and reliable workflow to keep you focused and productive.
​✨ Key Features
​⏱️ Time Tracking & Monitoring: Easily keep track of time spent on tasks, projects, or daily activities.
​🔔 Smart Alerts & Reminders: Get notified when time limits are reached or scheduled intervals end.
​📊 Activity Insights: Review your time usage logs to identify habits and boost productivity.
​⚡ Lightweight & Fast: Minimal resource consumption with a straightforward user experience.
​🔧 Customizable Rules: Set personalized timers, schedules, or restrictions tailored to your routine.
